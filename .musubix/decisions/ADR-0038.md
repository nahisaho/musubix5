# ADR-0038: Wrap immutable Generation-10 payloads with fresh Generation-11 authority

## Context

CHANGE-0017 Generation 10 implemented and tested the recovery payload, but its
Red, Implementation and Green change checkpoints were appended after the TDD
orders they were meant to bound. Append-only chronology cannot repair that
ordering in place. Reusing those checkpoints or TDD cycles as current evidence
would make a structurally invalid history appear successful.

The payload also retains useful compatibility identities and canonical P10
schemas. Renaming or regenerating every P10 byte solely because the lifecycle
generation advanced would create unnecessary compatibility risk and would not
repair the chronology.

## Decision

Abandon Generation 10 as immutable non-credit history and continue in
Generation 11 with fresh requirements/design approvals and correctly ordered
checkpoints. Introduce DES-M5-025 and a closed RecoveryAuthority11 wrapper plus
AuthoritySeal11. Bootstrap approvals/checkpoints bind Generation 11 and the
exact Generation-10 abandonment directly; every later mutation binds the final
wrapper and seal.

Delegate immutable P10 payload schemas, identities, state matrix and accounting
base to DES-M5-024, but do not treat RecoveryAuthority10 or any Generation-10
phase/TDD evidence as Generation-11 authority. Publish one immutable
repository-root custody copy of RecoveryAuthority10, then the wrapper and seal.
Add nine explicitly charged custody/wrapper/seal paths, raising the exact
maximum from 534118400 to 535298048 bytes. Require ten fresh named Red/Green cycles and repair the remaining
release-only, durable-abort, exact-D1-delta, accounting-latch and fence-chain
gaps before quality.

## Rejected alternatives

- Waive the late Generation-10 checkpoints: rejected because chronology is the
  proof boundary and a waiver would transfer unbounded TDD credit.
- Rewrite or delete prior order/change evidence: rejected because evidence is
  append-only and historical bytes must remain reviewable.
- Rename every G10/P10 payload kind and path to G11: rejected because generation
  authority belongs in the outer wrapper and byte churn would add compatibility
  risk without improving chronology.
- Let current approval files implicitly authorize the payload: rejected because
  the exact abandonment, workspace, baseline and manifest identities would be
  unsealed.

## Consequences

Generation 11 requires a new explicit requirements and design approval before
fresh Red. The implementation gains one acyclic wrapper module and nine
accounted publication paths. Existing G10 tests remain payload-level regression
tests only; ten new tests provide current lifecycle credit. Release can prove
the full monotonic order without modifying or borrowing Generation-10 evidence.
